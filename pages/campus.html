JVM Shyamali Athletes Shine at CBSE Cluster III Athletics Meet 2025

All medallists qualified for the national meet (10–13 Sep 2025, Varanasi). Principal Mr. Samarjit Jana congratulated the winners.

Legacy Since 1966

Sixty Years of Nurturing Excellence

Jawahar Vidya Mandir (JVM), Shyamali was founded in 1966 under the patronage of Jawahar Vidya Mandir Education Society and MECON Limited in Doranda, Ranchi. Situated in the serene, tree-lined Shyamali residential colony, the institution has flourished into one of Eastern India's most prestigious centers of primary and senior secondary education.

The school holds permanent affiliation with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi (Affiliation No. 3430004, School Code: 66230), imparting quality education from Nursery to Class XII across Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams.

Admission procedure

  1. Identify the applicant's class and, for Class 11, whether the applicant is an internal or external student and the intended stream.
  2. Review the relevant eligibility criteria and any provisional test requirement below.
  3. Prepare the default documents listed below where a class-specific list is available.
  4. Visit the school office through Gate No. 3 for the current application process, availability, official dates, fees and any additional instructions.

This guidance does not establish an exact deadline, application venue beyond the office direction below, syllabus or guaranteed admission.

What are the daily operating school hours for students?

• Senior Wing (Classes VI to XII): 06:55 AM – 01:10 PM
• Primary Wing (Classes I to V): 08:45 AM – 02:45 PM

What streams and electives are offered in Senior Secondary (XI & XII)?

Science (PCM/PCB with CS, AI, Biotech, or PE), Commerce (with Applied Mathematics or IP), and Humanities (History, Political Science, Economics, Psychology, and Sociology).
